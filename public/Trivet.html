Trivet

A decorative coaster inspired by the triskele symbol, combining functional surface protection with a contemporary interpretation of Celtic visual language.

Project

Coaster Design

Focus

Product Design, CAD Modelling, Packaging

Inspiration

Celtic symbolism and everyday ritual objects.

A contemporary interpretation of a traditional symbol.

Trivet explores how cultural motifs can be translated into modern domestic products. The triskele-inspired geometry functions as both ornament and structure, creating a product intended for everyday use while maintaining a strong visual identity.
