AR Navigation Eyewear for Winter Environments

Navigate Without Looking Down.

USK integrates augmented reality navigation directly into the wearer's field of view, providing route guidance and environmental awareness in snow-covered terrain. Designed for outdoor exploration, the system allows users to remain focused on their surroundings while receiving real-time navigational support.

Key Features

Real-Time Navigation

Live directional guidance within the user’s field of view.

Heads-Up Route Guidance

Follow routes without relying on handheld devices.
