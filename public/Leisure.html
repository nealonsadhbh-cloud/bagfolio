Project

Leisure Category Branding

Client

Choice Stores

Focus

Branding, Signage & Environmental Graphics

Overview

The Leisure category identity was developed for Choice Stores to create a distinctive retail experience across multiple customer touchpoints. The project included category branding, large-scale signage, environmental graphics and wayfinding systems designed to increase visibility and create a stronger in-store presence.

Environmental Graphics

A flexible visual system was developed to work across storefronts, category signage, promotional graphics and wayfinding elements. The resulting identity ensured consistency throughout the customer journey while creating a clear visual distinction for the Leisure category.
